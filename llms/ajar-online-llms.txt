# Ajar Online

> Ajar Online is a cloud-based property management and rent-collection platform headquartered in Kuwait (founded 2015). It lets landlords, agencies, and property managers send rent requests over SMS and email and collect payment via cards, KNET, and Sadad, with portfolio dashboards, tenant/contract management, and analytics. It operates across Kuwait, the UAE, and Saudi Arabia.
